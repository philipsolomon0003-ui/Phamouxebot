Official Discord Policy: QR-Code & Self-Bot Notice

Discord does not officially support QR-code login for automating personal accounts or operating self-bots. Discord’s mobile QR protocol (/ra/remote-auth) is strictly an internal mechanism reserved for Discord’s official desktop application. Third-party applications simulating QR codes violate Discord’s Developer Terms, risk permanent account termination, and expose users to token theft. PHAMOUXE exclusively implements Discord’s official, supported OAuth2 Web Authorization protocol.
